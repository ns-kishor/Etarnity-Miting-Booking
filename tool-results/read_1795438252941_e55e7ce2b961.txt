    14→    14→# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
    15→    15→
    16→    16→## 1.1 Product Vision
    17→    17→
    18→    18→Develop a modern, premium, minimal and fully responsive online meeting booking platform for **Etarnity Global Innovation**.
    19→    19→
    20→    20→The platform will allow external visitors, potential clients, business partners, investors and other interested individuals to discover the company's board members, explore their professional expertise, check their availability and book meetings directly with their preferred board member.
    21→    21→
    22→    22→Additionally, each board member will have an individual dashboard to manage meeting requests, availability schedules and personal profiles.
    23→    23→
    24→    24→A Super Admin will have complete administrative control over the entire platform.
    25→    25→
    26→    26→## 1.2 Primary Objectives
    27→    27→
    28→    28→- Make meeting booking simple and accessible.
    29→    29→- Showcase the company's board members professionally.
    30→    30→- Eliminate double bookings and scheduling conflicts.
    31→    31→- Give board members independent meeting management capabilities.
    32→    32→- Provide centralized control through a Super Admin panel.
    33→    33→- Deliver a premium user experience across all devices.
    34→    34→
    35→    35→## 1.3 User Roles
    36→    36→
    37→    37→The application will have three primary user roles:
    38→    38→
    39→    39→| Role | Responsibilities |
    40→    40→|---|---|
    41→    41→| Visitor / Client | Discover board members and book meetings |
    42→    42→| Board Member | Manage bookings, schedules and profile |
    43→    43→| Super Admin | Manage the entire platform |

   160→   160→## 3.2 Availability Calendar
   161→   161→
   162→   162→Create an interactive calendar.
   163→   163→
   164→   164→### Calendar Requirements
   165→   165→
   166→   166→- Display current month and year.
   167→   167→- Previous and next month navigation.
   168→   168→- Highlight available dates.
   169→   169→- Disable unavailable dates.
   170→   170→- Disable past dates.
   171→   171→- Clearly distinguish the selected date.
   172→   172→- Display availability using subtle indicators.
   173→   173→- Prevent selection of dates outside the booking window.
   174→   174→
   175→   175→When a visitor selects a date, the available time slots for that date must appear dynamically.
   176→   176→
   177→   177→## 3.3 Available Time Slots
   178→   178→
   179→   179→Display time slots on the right side of the calendar.
   180→   180→
   181→   181→Example:
   182→   182→
   183→   183→**Monday, December 07**
   184→   184→
   185→   185→Available time slots:
   186→   186→
   187→   187→- 10:00 AM — Available
   188→   188→- 11:00 AM — Time Already Booked
   189→   189→- 02:00 PM — Available
   190→   190→- 03:00 PM — Available
   191→   191→
   192→   192→### Slot Statuses
   193→   193→
   194→   194→Every slot must have a clear status:
   195→   195→
   196→   196→- Available — Selectable.
   197→   197→- Pending — Temporarily unavailable while a booking request is being processed.
   198→   198→- Booked — Not selectable.
   199→   199→- Unavailable — Not selectable due to board member's schedule.
   200→   200→
   201→   201→Booked slots must display:
   202→   202→
   203→   203→`Time Already Booked`
   204→   204→
   205→   205→Do not allow visitors to select occupied time slots.
   206→   206→
   207→   207→### Critical Booking Logic
   208→   208→
   209→   209→Implement server-side booking validation.
   210→   210→
   211→   211→The system must:
   212→   212→
   213→   213→1. Fetch availability from the database.
   214→   214→2. Check existing bookings.
   215→   215→3. Temporarily reserve a selected slot during the booking process.
   216→   216→4. Prevent simultaneous booking attempts from claiming the same slot.
   217→   217→5. Revalidate availability when the visitor submits the form.
   218→   218→6. Release expired temporary reservations automatically.
   219→   219→
   220→   220→Use database transactions or equivalent atomic locking to guarantee that two visitors cannot successfully reserve the same board member's time slot.
   221→   221→
   222→   222→**Important:** A pending booking request must also block the corresponding slot until it is approved, rejected or expires, according to the configured booking policy.
   223→   223→
   224→   224→## 3.4 Meeting Method Selection
   225→   225→
   226→   226→Display available meeting methods in the left section.
   227→   227→
   228→   228→Possible options:
   229→   229→
   230→   230→- Google Meet
   231→   231→- Zoom
   232→   232→- Microsoft Teams
   233→   233→- Phone Call
   234→   234→- Physical / In-person Meeting
   235→   235→
   236→   236→These options must be configurable by individual board members.
   237→   237→
   238→   238→A visitor can select only the methods enabled by the relevant board member.
   239→   239→
   240→   240→If a meeting method requires additional information, display the appropriate conditional fields.
   241→   241→
   242→   242→For example, an in-person meeting may require a preferred location.
   243→   243→
   244→   244→## 3.5 Booking Steps
   245→   245→
   246→   246→Implement a three-step booking flow.
   247→   247→
   248→   248→**Step 01 — Select Date & Time**
   249→   249→
   250→   250→**Step 02 — Complete Your Details**
   251→   251→
   252→   252→**Step 03 — Confirmation**
   253→   253→
   254→   254→The Next button should remain disabled until all required selections are completed.
